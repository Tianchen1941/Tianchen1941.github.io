The relevance of primes in the Brauer–Manin obstruction

Study group at ICERM · Fall 2026 · Organized by Tianchen Zhao

About

Which primes can contribute to the Brauer–Manin obstruction on a variety over a number field? This study group goes through recent results on this question, with an emphasis on K3 surfaces. There are also talks on Brauer–Manin obstruction not directly related to the relevance of primes.
